 Deck and Cards

 
 
 Before a battle, collect your own unique deck in the Deck Editor or pick one of the ready-to-play decks available in the Garage .

 
 
 
 
 
 Every deck contains 41 cards; no more, no less. Every deck has only one Headquarters (or HQ). The other 40 cards are units, squads, and orders.

 The HQ is the key card of the deck. Once your HQ is destroyed, the game is over.

 Units are divided into five types: light, medium, and heavy tanks, as well as artillery (or SPG) and tank destroyers.

 Squads are infantry forces that support your HQ.

 Orders are special action cards with a one-time effect.
