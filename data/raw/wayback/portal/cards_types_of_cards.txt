 Types of Cards

 
 
 There are four types of cards in World of Tanks Generals:

 
 
 
 Headquarters 
 Units 
 Squads 
 Orders 

 
 
 
 
 

 
 The main card of the deck. 
 Battlefield action units. 
 Infantry forces that support the HQ 
 Special action cards with a one-time effect. 

 
 
 
 
 
 Cards may have additional attributes . Some attributes only work if the HQ is of a certain nation or type. 

 
 
 

 
 Headquarters

 The HQ is your most important card and there is only one in each deck. If your HQ is destroyed, you lose the battle.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 HQs are divided into four types:

 
 
 
 Symbol 
 HQ Type 
 Description 

 
 
 Training HQ 
 Several basic HQs are available to all players. 

 
 
 Assault HQ 
 Increased firepower. 

 
 
 Support HQ 
 Increased resource growth. 

 
 
 Consolidated HQ 
 Increased hit points. 

 
 
 

 HQs have the following characteristics:

 

 
 
 
 The HQ&rsquo;s nation and type can affect some card attributes . Consider this when collecting your new deck. 

 
 
 

 
 Units

 Units are the main action cards of the game. Units represent tanks, artillery, and tank destroyers. They move on the Battlefield, attack your enemy, and make the most impact on the Battle Screen.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 Units are divided into five types:

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 Light tanks 
 Medium tanks 
 Heavy tanks 
 Artillery (or SPGs) 
 Tank destroyers 

 
 
 

 Units have the following characteristics:

 

 The way units move, attack, and counterattack depends on their type. See also Moving Units and Attacking and Counterattacking . 

 
 Squads

 Squads are infantry forces that support the HQ. Squads are placed on the HQ Support Panel and cannot move. Some squads help you attack your opponent while others help you defend your HQ.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 
 

 Squads are divided into two types:

 
 
 
 Symbol 
 Squad Type 
 Description 

 
 
 
 

Assault Squad 
 When your HQ attacks, this squad increases the HQ&rsquo;s firepower by the value of the squad strength. At the same time, the squad receives damage equal to its strength .
 e.g. The HQ&rsquo;s firepower is 1, the assault squad strength is 2. The squad increases the HQ&rsquo;s firepower by 2 and loses 2 HP. The total firepower is 3. 

 
 
 
 

Defensive Squad 
 When your HQ is attacked, this squad absorbs part of the damage. The amount of damage absorbed cannot exceed the value of the squad strength.
 e.g. The defensive squad strength is 2. If the enemy attack is 1, the squad loses 1 HP; if the attack is 2, the squad loses 2 HP; if the attack is 3, the squad loses 2 HP and the HQ loses 1 HP. 

 
 
 

 Squads are also divided into five specializations:

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 
 Reconnaissance squad 
 Communication squad 
 Artillery squad 
 Medical squad 
 Engineer squad 

 
 
 
 
 
 You can have only one squad of each specialization in the HQ Support Panel at one time. If you deploy a second squad of the same specialization, the first squad is destroyed and put into Casualties.

 Orders

 Orders are special action cards. Orders produce their effect immediately after deployment and then go to Casualties.
