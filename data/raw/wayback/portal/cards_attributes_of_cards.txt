 Attributes of Cards

 
 
 A number of cards in World of Tanks Generals have special attributes that give them a unique strategic purpose.
These attributes describe additional rules for the cards.

 There are several types of attributes:

 
 Key Attributes 

 Dependent Attributes 

 Activated Attributes 

 Paid Attributes 
 

 
 

 
 Key Attributes

 
 
 
 Key attributes have their own names, which can be found on the cards. 
 


 
 
 
 
 
 Attribute 
 Description 


 
 Rearguard 
 The card can be deployed for 0 resources if it is the last card in your hand. 


 
 Blitz 
 Rather than pay with resources, you can pay with the same number of random cards from your deck, which are put into Casualties. 


 
 Mass Production 
 You can add three or more copies of this card to your deck.
 Number of copies = HQ tier + 3. 


 
 Camouflage 
 The card cannot be attacked by your enemy&rsquo;s HQ or SPG even when spotted. 


 
 Defense 
 When your HQ is attacked, this card absorbs all damage if the card is on the Bridgehead. 


 
 Cover 
 The card is not subject to counterattack. 


 
 Reinforcements 
 After playing this card, you receive an additional card in your hand. 


 
 
 
 
 

 


 
 
 

 
 Dependent Attributes

 
 
 
 Dependent attributes only work if your HQ belongs to a certain nation and/or type. 
 


 
 
 
 
 
 Symbol 
 Description 


 
 
 The attribute works for assault HQs. 


 
 
 The attribute works for support HQs. 


 
 
 The attribute works for consolidated HQs. 


 
 
 The attribute works for USSR HQs. 


 
 
 The attribute works for Germany HQs. 


 
 
 The attribute works for USA HQs. 


 
 
 During battle, you see the attributes that work with your current HQ nation and type. You can view all card attributes in the Deck Editor .

 
 
 

 


 
 
 

 
 Activated Attributes

 
 
 
 
 Some cards have an activated attribute that can be used once per turn instead of attack.

Activated attributes are marked with a special symbol with two arrows. 
 
 

 


 
 
 

 
 Paid Attributes

 
 
 
 
 Some cards have a paid attribute that can be used several times during your turn. To activate this attribute, you pay resources equal to the number on the jerrycan.

Paid attributes are marked with a jerrycan.
