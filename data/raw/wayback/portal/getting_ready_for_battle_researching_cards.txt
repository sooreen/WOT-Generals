 Researching Cards

 
 
 In every battle you receive credits and experience that you can use to research and buy new cards .

 
 
 
 
 
 To research a card: 

 
 In the Garage, click Research to the right of the BATTLE! button.

 Click the required nation flag and HQ. The research tree for the selected HQ opens.

 Click the card you want to research.

 
 
 
 You cannot research a card if the previous card in the branch is not researched. 


 
 


 Click Research .

 
 
 


 
 
 
 
 
 Click Purchase to purchase the researched card immediately and add it to your Collection . 


 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 To view the research tree for a certain nation: 

 
 In the Garage, click Research to the right of the BATTLE! button.

 Click the required nation flag. The research tree for the selected nation opens.

 
 In the research tree, you can see several HQs available for the selected nation.


 
 
 


 
 
 

 
 
 
 
 
 To view the research tree for a certain nation: 

 
 In the Garage, click Research to the right of the BATTLE! button.

 Click the required nation flag. The research tree for the selected nation opens.

 Click the HQ. The research tree for the selected HQ opens.

 
 
 


 
 
 

 There are several card statuses in the research tree for the selected HQ:

 
 
 
 
 
 
 


 
 А. The card is not available for research&mdash;research its parent cards first. 
 B. The card is available for research. 
 C. The card is available for purchase. The number of copies you bought is indicated on the lower right corner. 
 D. The card is not available for purchase&mdash;you bought the maximum number of cards.
